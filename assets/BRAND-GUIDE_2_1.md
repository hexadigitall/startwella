# StartWella Brand — v2 WHITE-FIRST (App-Standard)

## Rule
White is primary. Either white as background (coloured mark on white tile) OR white as subject (white mark on teal tile). Never a dark navy tile as primary — it disappears in dark mode.

## Primary Logo
- White rounded-square tile, thin light-grey border
- Teal W #0EA47A, Gold dot #F2B705
- Use on light, dark, colourful wallpapers — white tile always pops.

## Secondary Logo
- White W + gold dot on teal tile #0EA47A — for dark headers, splash, favicon on dark mode.
- Old navy tile (logo-v1-navy-alt.png) is DEPRECATED — do not use as primary.

## Colours
- White #FFFFFF (primary background)
- Teal #0EA47A (mark / actions)
- Navy #0F2A44 (wordmark / headings only, NOT tile)
- Gold #F2B705 (profit accent)
- Light #EAF6F1, Grey #5A6B7C, BG #F2F4F7

- logo-white-on-teal-1024.png = secondary
